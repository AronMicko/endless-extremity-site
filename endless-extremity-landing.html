PROLOGUE

Rulpio remembers every man who tried and failed

It is a land where sinners thrive, killers and traitors walking the same broken streets as the people they ruined. Ravel arrives with an impossible order: organize Rulpio, and make it the center of unity again.

"Travel at the right time," says a man lying near the carriage road. He is blind, his face veiled, and he already knows who Ravel is.

What you say to him, and every stranger after him, is never just dialogue. It is the first thread of a story that only you will finish.

CHAPTER I · THE RECOGNITION

Every line of dialogue is a decision

There is no correct script to memorize. Caution, pride, mercy, and force all open different doors, and the game remembers which one you walked through. Some choices cost you points. Others cost you allies.

Nothing is undone quietly. The story folds your decision back into itself and keeps moving.

WHAT YOU CARRY WITH YOU

Built for a story you keep coming back to

Points and rank, earned honestly

Every decision carries a point value. Climb from an unnamed soldier to a name Rulpio will not forget, and watch your rank color change as you go.

Unranked Soldier Officer Legend

Achievements that interrupt you

A quiet toast slides in the moment you earn one, mid scene, without pulling you out of the chapter you're in.

Share your rank card

Your progress becomes a single shareable card, shaded in your current rank color, ready to send to whoever doubts your run.

A score that follows the mood

An original ambient and percussion score plays through the entire session, shifting with the weight of the chapter.

Offline from the first chapter

No login, no connection required. Rulpio loads the moment you open the app and stays with you without a signal.
